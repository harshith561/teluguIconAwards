Results Transparency

Transparent Voting. Protected Final Results.

The website must show the public exactly what is visible during each stage and publish the final verified results after the live announcement.

10 OCT – 11 NOV

Live Public Results. Verified vote totals, category positions and last-updated information may be displayed publicly for the 50 voting categories.

12 NOV – 15 NOV

Vote Display Hidden. Voting remains fully open, but public vote totals, rankings and leaderboard counts are hidden to avoid influencing late-stage voting.

15 NOV · 11:59 PM

Voting Locked. No new votes are accepted after the published closing time. The system begins final reconciliation and verification.

16 NOV · LIVE EVENT

Winners Announced. Winners are revealed on stage at Shilpakala Vedika, Hyderabad. The website must not publish winners early.

AFTER ANNOUNCEMENT

Final Results Page. Publish the verified winner, final verified vote total for each public-voting category, nominee ID and relevant verification/audit information.

JURY AWARDS

No Public Vote Totals. The 10 Jury & Special Recognition Awards are clearly identified as jury/special awards and are not part of public voting.

Audit & Integrity Record

Maintain secure vote records, timestamps, verification status, duplicate/fraud review records and admin audit logs according to the site's published privacy and data-retention policy. Public users see the transparent final outcome; sensitive fraud-detection signals remain protected.

Need To Know

Frequently Asked Questions.

When is Telugu Icon Digital & Influencer Awards 2026?

November 16, 2026 at Shilpakalavedika, Hyderabad.

Is this a cinema or OTT awards event?

No. The current event is specifically a Digital & Influencer Awards event focused on Telugu creators and digital influence.

When do nominations open?

Self-nominations open October 1, 2026.

Can I nominate someone else?

No. Only self-nominations are accepted.

When does public voting open?

Voting opens October 10, 2026.

Will vote counts be visible?

Yes. Verified public vote counts and category positions will be displayed during the voting period.

When does voting close?

November 13, 2026 at 11:59 PM.

Are tickets refundable?

Tickets are non-refundable and non-cancellable except where a refund is required due to event cancellation or applicable law.

What are the two event sessions?

Session 1 is 10 AM–2 PM with complimentary lunch. Session 2 is 3 PM–7 PM with complimentary dinner.

Will the website show the number of seats remaining?

No. The public ticketing interface will show only availability status such as Available, Filling Fast, Almost Sold Out or Sold Out.
